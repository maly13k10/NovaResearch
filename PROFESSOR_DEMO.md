# NovaResearch AI — Professor / Jury Demo

## Suggested 7–10 minute demo
1. **Competition Dashboard** — show the project at a glance: readiness, novelty, best ML model, privacy candidate, robustness, top feature, reproducibility and next action.
2. **Project AI** — upload a PDF and show automatic domain/readiness analysis plus Paper DNA.
3. **Research Ideas + Comparison Arena** — show multiple research directions and select one globally.
4. **Novelty & Gap Lab** — run the Crossref candidate search, Novelty Radar, Heatmap and Gap Matrix.
5. **Experiment Planner** — generate a reproducible protocol.
6. **ML & Privacy Lab** — show benchmark models, Differential Privacy epsilon sweep and Privacy Advisor.
7. **Robustness & XAI** — show noise/missingness, automatic ablation, sensor drift, SHAP global/local and exact McNemar validation.
8. **Ask Nova** — ask a contextual follow-up such as “Why is this the next step?” or “What evidence in my PDF supports this?”.
9. **Research Graph + Reproducibility Passport** — show the complete research flow and evidence status.
10. **Publication Studio** — export the DOCX draft and complete publication ZIP.

## What is distinctive
NovaResearch AI does not stop at PDF summarization. It connects project understanding with research-design decisions, reproducibility, privacy, robustness, explainability, statistical validation and publication-oriented exports.

## Distribution
Provide the professor with:
- the public online URL after deployment;
- `NovaResearchAI.exe` / Windows professor ZIP after build;
- optionally the source ZIP for reproducibility.
